GymFlow Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 8, 2024
Last Updated: November 8, 2024

1. Introduction

Welcome to GymFlow ("we," "our," or "us"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application GymFlow (the "App"). Please read this privacy policy carefully. If you do not agree with the terms of this privacy policy, please do not access the App.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Personal Information You Provide

  • Account Information: When you create an account, we collect your email address and display name
  • Profile Information: Information you choose to add to your profile
  • Gym Reports: Crowd level reports you submit about gym zones and facilities
  • Favorites: Gyms you mark as favorites for quick access

2.2 Information Automatically Collected

  • Device Information: Device type, operating system, app version
  • Usage Analytics: How you interact with the App (via Firebase Analytics)
  • Crash Reports: Technical information when the App experiences issues (via Firebase Crashlytics)
  • Location Data: Approximate location to suggest nearby gyms (only when permission granted)

2.3 Information from Third Parties

  • Firebase Authentication: We use Google Firebase for secure user authentication
  • Social Login: If you choose to sign in with Google, Apple, or other providers, we receive basic profile information

3. How We Use Your Information

3.1 Core App Functionality

  • User Authentication: Secure login and account management
  • Crowd Reports: Display community-reported gym crowd levels
  • Personalization: Show your favorite gyms and personalized recommendations
  • Data Sync: Keep your preferences synced across devices

3.2 App Improvement

  • Analytics: Understand how users interact with the App to improve features
  • Bug Fixes: Identify and resolve technical issues
  • New Features: Develop features based on user behavior and feedback

3.3 Communication

  • Account Updates: Important changes to your account or the App
  • Customer Support: Respond to your questions and provide assistance

4. Information Sharing and Disclosure

4.1 Community Data Sharing

  • Crowd Reports: Your crowd level reports are aggregated and shared anonymously with other users
  • No Personal Identification: Reports cannot be traced back to individual users

4.2 Third-Party Service Providers

We share information with trusted third-party services:

  • Google Firebase: Cloud storage, authentication, analytics, and crash reporting
    Privacy Policy: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • Apple/Google App Stores: Basic usage statistics as required by app store policies

4.3 Legal Requirements

We may disclose your information if required by law or to:

  • Comply with legal processes
  • Protect our rights and safety
  • Investigate fraud or security issues
  • Protect other users' safety

5. Data Security

5.1 Security Measures

  • Encryption: All data transmitted is encrypted using industry-standard protocols
  • Firebase Security: We use Google Firebase's enterprise-grade security infrastructure
  • Access Controls: Strict access controls limit who can access user data
  • Regular Updates: We regularly update our security practices

5.2 Data Retention

  • Account Data: Retained while your account is active and for 30 days after deletion
  • Crowd Reports: Aggregated reports may be retained indefinitely for community benefit
  • Analytics Data: Retained according to Firebase Analytics retention policies (up to 26 months)

6. Your Privacy Rights

6.1 Access and Control

  • View Data: Access your personal information through the App settings
  • Update Information: Modify your profile and preferences at any time
  • Delete Account: Permanently delete your account and associated data
  • Export Data: Request a copy of your personal data

6.2 Communication Preferences

  • Opt-Out: Disable non-essential notifications in App settings
  • Marketing: We do not send marketing communications (App is community-focused)

6.3 Regional Rights

Depending on your location, you may have additional rights under laws such as:

  • GDPR (European Union): Right to access, rectify, erase, restrict, or port your data
  • CCPA (California): Right to know what data is collected and request deletion
  • Other Local Laws: Rights under applicable local privacy regulations

7. Children's Privacy

GymFlow is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we discover we have collected information from a child under 13, we will promptly delete such information.

8. International Data Transfers

Your information may be transferred to and processed in countries other than your country of residence. These transfers are conducted in accordance with applicable privacy laws and with appropriate safeguards in place.

  • Significant Changes: Provide additional notice for material changes
  • Continued Use: Your continued use constitutes acceptance of the updated policy
